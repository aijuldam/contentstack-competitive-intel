## 7. FAQ Copy for Launch Objections

**Q: What exactly do I get from the free plan?**
A: Framework guides for MEDDIC and Command of the Message, messaging templates, and example outputs from three sample companies. You can see exactly what the full workflow produces without committing to the paid plan. To run the workflow on your own product, you need the paid plan at €5/month.

**Q: Is €5/month a real price or a teaser rate that changes later?**
A: €5/month is the confirmed price for the full workflow. It is deliberately priced low for an early product — the intent is to remove price as a reason not to try it. This may change as the product matures, but existing subscribers will be notified well in advance.

**Q: What if the output is terrible?**
A: The output quality is directly proportional to the specificity of your inputs. If you give vague inputs, the system produces vague output and flags what's missing. The product is not magic — it is structure applied to what you provide. Gaps are listed explicitly so you can improve the intake and regenerate.

**Q: Will it work for my product category?**
A: Go-to-Market Taste is built for B2B SaaS products with a defined commercial buyer, a sales process, and a measurable business outcome. It works best when you can answer all four intake questions with meaningful specifics. It is not optimized for consumer apps, developer tools without a business buyer, or marketplace products.

**Q: Is my data used to train AI models?**
A: No. Your intake and generated content are never used to train AI models. All data is private to your account.

**Q: What is the catch with €5/month?**
A: There is no catch. It is early software with an MVP scope. Stripe checkout is not yet live — billing will be activated shortly. PDF and PPTX exports are coming but not available now. HTML export is available. The framework, intake, Messaging Foundation, and asset generation all work.
