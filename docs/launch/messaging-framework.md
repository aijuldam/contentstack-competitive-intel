## The Product Truth

Before positioning comes honesty about what the product does right now:

**What it does:**
- Accepts four plain-language intake fields about a B2B SaaS product
- Runs them through a structured AI pipeline grounded in MEDDIC and Command of the Message
- Produces a Messaging Foundation — a structured narrative where every claim is tagged verified (you said it) or inferred (the model derived it)
- Derives three assets from that approved foundation: a pitch deck, a one-pager, and a sales enablement deck
- Lets you edit every section, track versions, and export as HTML

**What it does not do (honest for launch):**
- It does not replace a PMM for complex enterprise positioning work
- It does not write for consumer products — it is built around a commercial B2B buyer model
- It does not invent metrics or fabricate customer proof — gaps are surfaced as missing, not filled in
- Stripe checkout is not yet live (honest placeholder in UI)
- PDF/PPTX export is not yet available

## Why Now Angle

B2B SaaS founders and PMMs have two bad options for messaging right now:
1. Spend weeks with a consultant or agency building a messaging house — expensive, slow, often founder-divorced.
2. Use a generic AI writing tool and get polished sentences that don't actually map to buyer pain, deal criteria, or differentiation.

Go-to-Market Taste is a third path: a structured, framework-grounded system that starts from what you actually know, surfaces what you haven't thought through yet, and produces output that AEs and founders can actually use in a real sales conversation.

The timing angle: AI writing tools are everywhere now. The problem is not that B2B teams can't generate text — it is that they generate text that doesn't hold up in a buyer conversation. The product is differentiated specifically because it is not trying to write for you — it is structuring what you already know.

---

## Three Key Value Points

**1. Structured, not generic**
The intake asks specific questions mapped to MEDDIC and Command of the Message. The output reflects what you actually know about your buyer, your pain, and your differentiation — not a rephrased product description.

**2. Honest about what the model doesn't know**
Every claim is tagged. Verified = you stated it. Inferred = the model derived it from your input. You see the difference before you approve anything. Gaps are listed as missing, not filled in with plausible-sounding content.

**3. One source, three aligned assets**
Your pitch deck, one-pager, and sales enablement deck all derive from the same approved Messaging Foundation. Change the source, regenerate an asset. No drift between documents. No copy-paste misalignment.

## Objection Handling

**"I can already do this with ChatGPT."**
You can generate text with ChatGPT. What you can't get from a general prompt is a structured output that maps every claim to MEDDIC elements, tells you which claims came from your input and which the model invented, and derives three consistent assets from a single approved source. The structure is the product.

**"I don't know MEDDIC / Command of the Message."**
You don't need to. The intake asks plain-language questions. The framework is the scaffolding the system applies — you fill in what you know about your product and buyer. Free plan includes the full framework guides if you want to understand what's happening under the hood.

**"We're not ready to formalize our messaging."**
That's exactly when to use it. The intake surfaces what you actually know, what you've assumed but haven't validated, and what's missing. That output is useful even before you start writing a pitch deck.

**"€5/month is too cheap for a B2B tool — is this really enterprise-ready?"**
This is an MVP with a clear scope. The pricing is intentional — low enough to not require procurement, high enough to filter out people who won't engage. It is not priced for enterprise. It is priced for founders, PMMs, and early-stage teams who need structured output fast.

**"I already have a PMM / messaging consultant."**
Use it as a fast-draft layer, not a replacement. PMMs can use Go-to-Market Taste to produce a structured first draft that the consultant refines, rather than starting from a blank deck.

## Differentiation Angle

**Against generic AI writing tools (Jasper, Copy.ai, generic GPT):**
Those tools generate text. Go-to-Market Taste generates structured, framework-grounded output from a specific intake, tells you what's verified vs inferred, and derives multiple aligned assets from one approved source. Different job.

**Against DIY frameworks (MEDDIC templates, messaging houses):**
Templates require you to fill in the framework yourself. Go-to-Market Taste applies the framework structurally to your inputs and produces a reviewable output in minutes. Less expertise required, more structure in the output.

**Against messaging consultants:**
Consultants are the right answer for complex enterprise positioning. Go-to-Market Taste is the right answer for early-stage teams that need structured output fast, can't afford six weeks, and want a working draft before bringing in human expertise.

**Against nothing (the current state for most):**
Most early-stage B2B teams have founder-verbal messaging that lives in no document or in a loose deck that's already out of sync. Go-to-Market Taste creates a written, structured, framework-aligned source that can actually be shared, revised, and used.
