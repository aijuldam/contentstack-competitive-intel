# Outreach Sequences — Go-to-Market Taste

Five sequences for founder-led distribution. All copy is short, human, and optimized
for conversation and learning — not conversion volume.

---

## 1. Warm Intro / Founder Network Outreach

**Use for:** People you know personally or have met professionally. PMMs, founders, GTM leads.
**Goal:** Get them into the product; get honest feedback.
**Channel:** LinkedIn DM or direct email.

Would love your reaction if you have 10 minutes to try the full workflow. The most useful thing for me right now is seeing where the output doesn't hold up.

## 2. Direct Outreach to Likely PMM Users

**Use for:** Product marketers at B2B SaaS companies you've identified, but don't know personally.
**Goal:** Get a trial user who will give candid feedback.
**Channel:** LinkedIn — keep it short and specific.

---

**Message 1:**

Hi {name},

I saw you work on product marketing at {company}. I built something I think fits that role — Go-to-Market Taste turns a four-field intake into a MEDDIC + Command of the Message narrative, then derives a pitch deck, one-pager, and sales deck from it. One approved source, three aligned assets.

It's early and I'm looking for PMMs who'd be willing to run their own product through it and tell me what the output gets wrong. Full workflow at €5/month, free framework guides available.

Worth trying?

**Follow-up (5 days, no reply):**

Hi {name},

Following up briefly — if you tried Go-to-Market Taste, I'd love your reaction. If not, no pressure. I'm specifically trying to find people who work on this kind of messaging so I can understand where the output falls short.

Even a "the Identify Pain section was too generic for our product" reply is useful.

## 3. Follow-up After Signup but No Activation

**Use for:** People who signed up (email captured) but haven't created a project.
**Goal:** Get them through the core workflow.
**Channel:** In-app nudge + lifecycle email (see lifecycle-messaging-map.md).
**Timing:** 24 hours after onboarding completed, no project created.

---

## 4. Follow-up After No Reply (General)

**Use for:** Any outreach where you haven't heard back after one follow-up.
**Goal:** One more low-pressure touchpoint, then close the loop.
**Channel:** Same channel as original outreach.
**Timing:** 7 days after first follow-up.

---

Hi {name},

Last note on this — if Go-to-Market Taste doesn't fit what you're working on right now, no problem.

If you did try it and had a reaction — positive or negative — I'd genuinely find that useful. You don't have to be diplomatic about it.

I'll leave it here. Good luck with {company/project if known}.

## 5. Feedback Request After First Use

**Use for:** Users who have opened at least one asset (activation milestone reached).
**Goal:** Get specific product feedback; identify testimonial candidates.
**Channel:** Direct email or LinkedIn. Do NOT send via automation for the first 20 users — send personally.
**Timing:** 48 hours after first asset opened.

---

Hi {first_name},

Looks like you've been through the full Go-to-Market Taste workflow — intake, foundation, and assets.

I'd genuinely like to know what the output got wrong.

Specifically:
- Which sections felt too generic or didn't reflect your actual product?
- Was there a place where the model made an inference you had to correct?
- Did the sales enablement deck have anything actually useful for an AE conversation, or did it feel off?
- What would need to be different for you to keep paying €5/month past the first month?

You can reply directly to this email. Even two sentences is useful.

If you're open to a 15-minute call instead, I'll take it. I'm not trying to sell you on anything — I want to understand what the product actually produces vs what the pitch promises.

Thanks for trying it.

## Notes on Outreach Sequencing

**Volume targets for week 1:**
- Warm network: 20–30 personal messages
- Direct PMM outreach: 10–15 people (quality over quantity)
- Total target signups from outreach: 10–20

**What to track per outreach:**
- Sent date
- Channel (LinkedIn / email)
- Response (yes / no / maybe)
- Signed up (yes / no)
- Activated (project created: yes / no)
- Feedback received (yes / no)

Keep a simple spreadsheet. Do not use a CRM for the first 50 outreaches — it adds friction and slows down iteration.

**First 10 activations are worth more than 100 signups.**
Prioritize getting the right people through the workflow and talking to them. Do not optimize for volume in week 1.
